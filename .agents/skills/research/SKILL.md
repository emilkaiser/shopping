# Product research skill

Use for deeper research within an existing purchase.

## Evidence order

1. Manufacturer / official technical documentation for specifications.
2. Independent professional reviews for testing and comparisons.
3. Retailers for price, condition, availability, warranty, and bundle details.
4. Community sources for recurring real-world strengths, annoyances, reliability patterns, sizing/fit, and edge cases.

## Rules

- Date-stamp time-sensitive conclusions.
- Keep source URLs beside the claim they support.
- Mark uncertainty explicitly.
- Normalize variants before comparing price.
- Prefer Swedish/EU market evidence unless the purchase says otherwise.
- Include shipping and known mandatory fees in effective price when practical.
- Do not treat MSRP as market value.
- Do not use a single reviewer or retailer as sufficient evidence for broad claims.

## Output

Update `research.md` with:
- findings that answer active questions;
- a compact comparison;
- notable evidence and sources;
- remaining uncertainties;
- recommendation impact.

Update `purchase.yaml` only for structured state that should drive tooling.
