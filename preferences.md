# Shopping preferences

These are defaults. Individual purchase records override them.

## Market

- Default region: Sweden.
- Default currency: SEK.
- Prefer Sweden/EU availability when practical.
- Compare effective price, including known shipping and mandatory fees.

## Value

- Used and refurbished products are welcome when condition, warranty, seller quality, and savings justify them.
- Prefer paying for meaningful capability rather than automatically choosing the newest generation.
- Treat unusually good previous-generation value as a first-class option.

## Research

Prioritize:
1. manufacturer specifications;
2. independent professional testing;
3. retailer offer details;
4. owner/community experience.

Keep objective facts separate from subjective experience.
