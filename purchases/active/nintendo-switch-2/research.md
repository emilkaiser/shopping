## Specifications and normalization

[Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/gaming-systems/switch-2/tech-specs/) lists a 7.9-inch 1080p LCD, 256 GB storage and approximately 2–6.5 hours battery life depending on use. Expansion for games requires [microSD Express](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Support/Nintendo-Switch-2/microSD-Card-and-microSD-Express-Card-Compatibility-2797377.html). Check compatibility for each existing game/accessory rather than assuming universal backward compatibility.

The [retailer package](https://www.elgiganten.se/product/gaming/spelkonsoler-tillbehor/nintendo-switch/nintendo-konsol/nintendo-switch-2-spelkonsol/913960) includes the dock, Joy-Con 2 pair, adapter and cables. Its 5,272 SEK figure is **excluding VAT**, not a consumer discount. Confirm EU region, delivery cost, stock, warranty and any used bundle code's redemption state. A bundle's title alone does not establish value.
