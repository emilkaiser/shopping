# Research

_Last updated: 2026-10-06_

## Current conclusion

**Verdict: buy at 7,490 SEK unless a materially cheaper comparable 42-inch OLED appears.**

The linked LG C5 42 fits the revised width constraint (~93 cm acceptable) and is a strong match for the intended bedroom use: OLED, compact footprint, excellent blacks, strong movie/streaming picture quality, and flexible wall-mount use.

The remaining research question is not whether the C5 is good, but whether another 42-inch OLED is enough cheaper or better to change the value decision.

## Review signal

### Professional review consensus

The LG C5 42 is generally rated very highly for:
- OLED black levels and contrast;
- strong HDR and SDR picture quality;
- motion handling;
- gaming features and low input lag;
- overall versatility as a compact premium TV.

Commonly noted drawbacks:
- the 42-inch model is not as bright as larger C5 sizes;
- built-in sound is ordinary rather than premium;
- the improvement over the C4 generation is relatively small.

That last point strengthens the value case only if a C4 can be found at a meaningfully lower price.

### Owner / community dimension

For this purchase, owner/community research should focus on:
- panel uniformity and near-black handling;
- reflections and bedroom viewing conditions;
- wall-mount ergonomics and cable access;
- long-term OLED/burn-in concerns in normal mixed use;
- whether owners perceive the C5 premium over C4 as worthwhile.

No recurring owner issue has yet been found that outweighs the current value case.

### Why it looks strong

At this price, the C5 sits low enough that a previous-generation C4 would need to be clearly cheaper to be the better buy. A newer-generation 42-inch OLED would need to land surprisingly close in price to offset the C5's value advantage.

## Decision rule

Buy the C5 unless one of these happens before purchase:

1. a comparable C4 / previous-gen OLED appears at a clearly lower effective price;
2. a current-generation alternative is within roughly 15-20% and offers meaningful improvements for bedroom/movie use;
3. a review-derived issue appears that materially affects wall-mount or bedroom suitability.
