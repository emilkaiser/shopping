# Decision

## Outcome

- Status: shortlist
- Product: LG C5 42
- Variant: 42-inch OLED, 2025
- Seller: Elgiganten
- Price: 7,490 SEK
- Date:

## Why

Current recommendation is to buy. The product fits the bedroom use case, the revised width constraint is acceptable, and the price is strong enough that obvious alternatives need to be materially cheaper or better to beat it.

## Main trade-off accepted

The C5 is only a modest step up from the C4 generation, so part of the value depends on the lack of a substantially cheaper C4/equivalent offer.

## What changed the decision

The 93 cm width is confirmed as acceptable, removing the only major fit concern.

## What to remember for future purchases

For compact premium TVs, generation-to-generation improvements matter less than relative street price once panel quality is already excellent.
