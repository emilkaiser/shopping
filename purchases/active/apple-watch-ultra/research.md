# Research

_Last updated: 2026-10-06_

## Current conclusion

The current value leader is **Apple Watch Ultra 2 refurbished**, but the present market is a **wait**, not an immediate buy.

A reputable Swedish reference point is reNewed at **4,880 SEK** for a 49 mm titanium Ultra 2 GPS + Cellular. That listing is currently temporarily out of stock and guarantees **at least 80% battery health**, **2 years warranty**, and **30-day free return**. At that price/battery floor, the deal is decent but not strong enough to force a purchase.

The practical alert levels are:

- **Ultra 2:** materially below **4,500 SEK**, or a somewhat higher price with clearly stronger individually stated battery health (preferably around 90%+).
- **Ultra 3:** around **6,000 SEK or below**, assuming reputable seller, good condition/warranty and Swedish-compatible cellular hardware.
- **Ultra 4:** only flag a material discount versus the 9,995 SEK new benchmark, or when the price gap to Ultra 3 becomes small enough that its battery/health improvements change the decision.

## Monitoring rule

Future checks should compare **total decision quality**, not just sticker price:

1. exact generation and 49 mm titanium GPS + Cellular variant;
2. Swedish/EU cellular compatibility;
3. stated battery health or battery guarantee;
4. cosmetic condition;
5. seller reputation;
6. warranty and return rights;
7. shipping/fees;
8. total effective price.

Notify only when a new offer can reasonably change the decision. Routine daily price checks should stay out of Git.

## Value framing

- Choose **Ultra 2 refurb** if savings remain large enough that newer battery/health features are not worth the gap.
- Choose **Ultra 3 refurb** if it reaches roughly 6,000 SEK and the unit has solid warranty/battery condition.
- Choose **Ultra 4 new** if the older generations stay too expensive, or its battery and newer health/recovery features become specifically important enough to justify the premium.
