## Current conclusion

The live decision is now **Ultra 2 refurb vs Ultra 3 refurb vs Ultra 4 new**.

Ultra 4 is the correct benchmark because it became the current model in September 2026. The value decision should therefore be based on how deeply Ultra 2 and Ultra 3 have discounted, rather than on which model is newest.

No buy recommendation yet: current Swedish refurb/used pricing for Ultra 2 and Ultra 3 still needs normalization by condition, warranty, seller, and variant.

## Findings

### Current generation

Apple introduced Apple Watch Ultra 4 on 2026-09-09 and began availability on 2026-09-18. The Swedish Apple Store currently lists it from 9,995 SEK.

### Meaningful Ultra 4 changes

Apple's current-generation changes are more substantial than a cosmetic refresh:
- new Health Sensing System;
- S11 chip;
- higher-frequency heart-rate and HRV measurements;
- readiness score;
- up to 50 hours normal battery life;
- up to 84 hours in Low Power Mode;
- up to 45 hours outdoor workout tracking in the maximum extended mode;
- faster charging, with Apple stating 15 minutes can add up to 18 hours of battery life.

These are meaningful specifically because battery and sports utility are high-priority criteria in this purchase.

### Value framing

Ultra 4 should not automatically win. The decision rule should be:

- choose **Ultra 2 refurb** if the savings are large enough that the newer health/readiness/battery improvements are not worth the gap;
- choose **Ultra 3 refurb** if its post-Ultra-4 discount creates the best middle ground;
- choose **Ultra 4 new** if previous generations remain too expensive relative to 9,995 SEK or if the new health/battery features prove especially valuable.

The next research pass should establish normalized Swedish market prices and define practical "good" and "great" price thresholds for Ultra 2 and Ultra 3.
