## Purchase / financing versus leasing

Compare the same 36-month period, annual distance, specification and running costs. Keep a separate longer ownership scenario if relevant. No actual loan approval, APR or user affordability is assumed.

| Route | Comparable cost over the holding period |
| --- | --- |
| Cash purchase | Purchase price − resale proceeds + running costs; show opportunity cost separately if included |
| Financed purchase | Purchase price − resale proceeds + interest actually paid + finance fees + running costs |
| Equivalent finance cash-flow check | Deposit + payments during holding period (principal and interest) + remaining debt settled on sale + fees + running costs − sale proceeds |
| Return-at-end lease | Initial fee + all rentals + mandatory fees + excluded running costs + excess-mileage/return charges; no resale proceeds |

Count each fee once. **Do not add interest again if it is already in monthly payments.** Include remaining loan/balloon debt in the cash-flow method; a deposit plus payments minus resale alone understates cost when debt remains. The older chat formula omitted this distinction and could double-count interest; the formulas above supersede it.

Illustration only: buy for 620,000 SEK, sell for 440,000 after three years, pay 65,000 interest/fees and 75,000 running costs → 320,000 SEK total, about 8,889/month. Vary resale ±60,000 → total 260,000–380,000, about 7,222–10,556/month. These are sensitivity inputs, not forecasts or current offers.

[Hyundai's page](https://www.hyundai.com/se/sv/bilar/ioniq9.html) advertises IONIQ 9 AWD Calligraphy Line leasing from 7,595 SEK/month, 36 months, 1,000 mil/year, zero deposit, service included. Rentals alone total 273,420 SEK. This is a useful quote lead, not a direct comparison with the illustration: confirm seats, availability, fees, insurance, winter wheels, mileage allowance, rate changes and return terms.

Prior financing suggestions—20–30% deposit and repayment over 4–5 years—remain discussion prompts, not selected terms. Compare effective APR, total credit cost and remaining balance on the same holding horizon. An estimated balloon is debt; it does not establish guaranteed buyback. [Konsumentverket](https://www.konsumentverket.se/fragor-och-svar/2955240/vad-menas-med-garanterat-restvarde-vid-privatleasing/) recommends checking what a lessor means by residual-value wording in the actual agreement.

[Konsumentverket's leasing guide](https://www.konsumentverket.se/varor-och-tjanster/leasa-bil-tips/) emphasizes contract terms, changes to fees, included services, mileage, return wear and early termination. Leasing can transfer resale exposure when the contract supports that, while still leaving usage and return costs. Buying may suit longer ownership; neither route wins without matched quotes and the user's mileage/horizon.

## Vehicle verification and evidence limits

[Kia Sweden's warranty terms](https://eu-www.kia.com/se/aga/garanti-assistans-forsakring/7-ars-nybilsgaranti/) describe seven years from first registration or 15,000 mil, with unlimited mileage during the first three years for private use and specific exclusions. Warranty follows the car subject to servicing conditions; imported vehicles need origin-country terms checked. The stated EV battery capacity threshold is below 70%. Verify the individual service/warranty book, registration date and remaining allowance rather than treating every component as covered for seven years.

For a candidate: check registered seat count, service history, recalls/campaign completion, battery/charging operation, tyres/winter wheels, brakes, suspension, accident history, towbar and seller fees. Review/owner reliability evidence has not been synthesized here; these are inspection tasks, not asserted defects. Confirm seven-person payload and luggage in person.
